EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Over this 3-day development sprint, the Somalia Blue Heaven platform underwent
a comprehensive visual, architectural, and performance overhaul. The platform
was transformed into an authentic, high-converting, luxury marine tourism and
conservation portal that directly satisfies all client criteria:

1. HOMEPAGE OCEAN IMAGE:
   - Replaced fragmented multi-pane grids and white dividing lines with an
     expansive, full-bleed cinematic hero background.
   - Preloaded high-priority responsive picture tags for instant, sharp rendering.

2. PREMIUM DESIGN SYSTEM:
   - Unified typography to modern, high-clarity sans-serif (Plus Jakarta Sans /
     Inter) across all 36 pages and components.
   - Established a cohesive luxury marine palette (Deep Navy #071B2B, Cerulean
     #0284c7, pristine whites #f8fafc, soft slate borders #e2e8f0).
   - Upgraded card elevations, pill badges, and glassmorphic micro-interactions.

3. AUTHENTIC DESTINATIONS (100% UNIQUE IMAGERY):
   - Audited all available local assets in public/ and distributed 5 unique,
     authentic field photographs to EVERY single one of the 17 destinations
     (85 distinct image assets in total, with 0 duplicates and 0 generic
     placeholder images).
   - Enriched 3D Cover Flow galleries in DestinationDetailPage.jsx with curated
     poetic narratives, precise GPS coordinates, and historical context.

4. OCEAN EXPERIENCES & ACTIVITIES:
   - Elevated the dedicated activities showcase highlighting Snorkeling, Scuba
     Diving, Dhow Tours, Underwater Photography, and Whale Shark Safaris.
   - Added an interactive 1-click filter tab system (All, Diving & Snorkeling,
     Dhow & Boat Tours, Marine Wildlife, Coast & Photography) for instant
     client-side exploration without page reload.

5. HIGH-CONVERTING BOOKING & CONTACT FLOW:
   - Engineered an interactive 4-step trip planning booking experience at
     /contact?booking=true (Expedition Type -> Destination & Dates -> Group
     Size & Style -> Passenger Contact Details).
   - Standardized all booking call-to-action buttons across the site from
     "Book a Seat" to the clearer, action-oriented "Book Trip Now".
   - Integrated operator trust badges (Verified Local Captains, Marine Biologist
     Led, Transparent Eco-Tours), emergency maritime hotlines, and WhatsApp.

6. MOBILE & SMALL SCREEN RESPONSIVENESS:
   - Full compatibility across viewports down to 375px (iPhone 7 Plus, SE,
     modern iOS/Android devices).
   - Replaced awkward rigid cutouts with floating glassmorphic luxury cards.
   - Fixed header drawer navigation, search input seams, and carousel swiping.

7. PERFORMANCE & EFFICIENCY (ZERO EXTRA HOSTING COSTS):
   - Clean 100% static Vite build deployed to Vercel with zero costly server
     dependencies or database bottlenecks.
   - Replaced heavy 3D canvas runtimes with hardware-accelerated CSS 3D
     transforms, slashing bundle size and mobile battery drain.
   - Production bundle builds in ~5.4 seconds with zero errors.
