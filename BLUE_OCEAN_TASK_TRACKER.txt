STATUS KEYS
  DONE     = Implemented and verified in the codebase
  PARTIAL  = Implemented but incomplete, or a close-but-not-exact match to spec
  MISSING  = Not found anywhere in the codebase

OVERALL SUMMARY
  - Core structure, navigation, homepage, regional coverage, research areas,
    conservation, coastal communities, and news are essentially complete and
    closely match the brief's wording.
  - The two recurring, cross-cutting gaps are:
      1) VIDEO support — every section of the brief asks for "photos AND
         video". The media pipeline (admin uploader + backend) only accepts
         images (jpeg/png/webp/gif) everywhere. No video upload/embed exists.
      2) Cross-linking "related news/articles" onto destinations, species,
         and research projects — not built.
  - Expeditions and the Marine Life "research observations" field are the
    least complete individual items (see sections 7 and 9).
